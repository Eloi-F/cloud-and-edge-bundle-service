The requested torque shall not exceed the maximum torque allowed by the powertrain.
Torque shall be increased only if the vehicle is in Drive or Rear mode.
Torque shall not be increased while the charging connector is attached.
Torque shall not be increased if the requested torque violates the current speed limits.
Torque shall not be increased if there is something detected near the vehicle.
Torque shall not be increased if the break are active
